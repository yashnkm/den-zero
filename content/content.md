*Proposed Registration Form Structure:*

*1. Team Details*

* Team size (1–3 members)
* Full name of each team member
* College name
* Course & year
* Email ID
* Phone number
* LinkedIn profile (optional)

*2. Team Lead Information*

* Primary contact person
* WhatsApp number
* Email address

*3. Startup / Idea Information*

* Startup or idea name
* Stage (Idea, Prototype/MVP, Early Startup, Revenue Generating)
* Problem statement
* Solution description
* Target audience
* What makes the idea unique?
* Current progress/status

*4. Pitch Deck Upload*

* Upload pitch deck (PDF)
* For teams that are still in the ideation stage and don't have a formal deck, we can allow a concept note or basic presentation.

*5. Payment Section*

* Registration fee: ₹499 per team
* Payment gateway/UPI
* Transaction ID/UTR number
* Payment screenshot upload

*6. Additional Information*

* How did you hear about Den Zero?
* What are you looking for from Den Zero?

  * Mentorship
  * Funding
  * Validation
  * Networking
  * Co-founders

*7. Declaration*

* Information provided is accurate
* Idea submitted is original
* Agreement to Den Zero's terms and evaluation process
